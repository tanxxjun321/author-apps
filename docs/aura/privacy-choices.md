# Privacy Choices

Last updated: April 24, 2026

This page explains how auralign users can manage privacy-related choices.

## 1. Local Data Management

You can manage data stored locally on your device in the following ways:

- Update or remove saved window layout presets in the App.
- Adjust App preferences and feature settings in the App.
- Uninstall the App to remove local data and caches associated with the App.

## 2. System Permissions

auralign may request system permissions, such as Accessibility permission, to provide window management features. You can review or revoke these permissions in your system settings. Some App features may not work if required permissions are disabled.

## 3. In-App Purchases

If you purchased premium features, you can use the "Restore Purchases" feature in the App to restore your entitlement. Related transaction records are managed by the Apple App Store.
