# Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to auralign (the "App"), a desktop window management app. We respect your privacy and aim to process as little data as reasonably possible.

## 1. Information We Process

The App's core features primarily rely on local processing on your device. Depending on the current version and the features you use:

- Window layout presets, workspace settings, and app preferences may be stored locally on your device.
- Some feature usage state and entitlement information may be stored locally on your device.
- The App may request system permissions, such as Accessibility permission, in order to read and manage windows as part of its window management features.
- The App may request network resources to load information about apps recommended by the author and related icons.
- In-app purchases are processed by the Apple App Store.

## 2. Local Storage

To provide its basic functionality, the App may store the following information locally on your device:

- Window layout presets and arrangement settings
- App preferences and configuration
- Feature usage state
- Cached static resources, such as images or icons

This information is used to support App functionality, improve performance, and provide a better user experience.

## 3. Network Requests

The App may use network requests to load limited public resources, such as:

- A list of apps recommended by the author
- Static resources such as recommended app icons

These requests are used only to display the corresponding features.

## 4. In-App Purchases

If you purchase premium features, transactions, payments, and order validation are handled by Apple. We do not directly receive your full payment information.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information when necessary to provide App functionality or as required by law.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. If a parent or guardian believes that a minor has submitted information that should not have been submitted, please contact us using the information below.

## 7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy based on product changes or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page.
